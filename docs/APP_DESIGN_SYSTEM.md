# Seatora Inside App Design System: "Case sheet" (Decide)

The landing page, privacy and auth pages use the dark roook-style system in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). Everything under `/decide` uses this one, a light, data-first system built on **[Astryx](https://github.com/meta/astryx)**, Meta's open-source design system (`@astryxdesign/core`, `theme-neutral`). The two never share a page.

## Why it looks like this
The user is a doctor working through a live counselling round, so the app reads like a hospital **case sheet**: chart paper, white sheets, navy ink, ruled hairlines, one chart-ink blue. Numbers are the content. They are set in **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** (Braille Institute), whose glyphs are built so that similar characters can't be confused, so a rank like 42,284 never reads as 42,824.

Black and white: the `/v2` home adds `mono` to the wrapper (`.app.mono`, globals.css). It's the same system in neutral greys, with black for ink, links and marks; signal colours keep their meaning. Fonts: `--font-app`, which is Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, used for body, headings and numbers alike. There's no pixel or monospace face inside the app.

## Colour key
Colour repeats what the words already say and never replaces them: a badge names its type, a cell shows its value, and the fee band is also in the cell's (i) and its screen-reader text. The key sits behind one (i), `ColourLegend` ("Colour key", colour-legend.tsx), on the institute page's key facts; a list page can put it beside its count. Each colour is at least 4.5:1 as text on its own 10% tint, on the sheet and on chart paper. The dark values (`.app.dark`, set on the `.app.mono.dark` theme) meet the same bar on black and on #111.

| What | Colour (words beside it) | Where it is set |
|---|---|---|
| College type | green state government + municipal, teal central government (AIIMS-type, armed forces, railway, PSU), violet ESIC, blue deemed, plum trust, grey private | `collegeTypeGroup` / `collegeTypeClass` (institute/college-type.ts), `TypeBadge` |
| Bond (years) | green "No bond" (a no-bond with a caveat stays ink), amber "1Y"/"2Y"/"Yes", orange 3Y or more, grey "Unknown"/"—" | `StatCell.level` (lib/stat-format.ts), `levelClass` (stat-cells.tsx) |
| Bond ₹, Leaving ₹ | red ₹25 L or more (`HIGH_PENALTY`), amber a smaller bond amount, green "None" | same |
| Fee a year | green low, ink middle, red high, by course type: MD/MS and Diploma under ₹1 L / from ₹5 L; DNB and NBEMS Diploma under / above the NBEMS maximum of ₹1.25 L. No band for an NBEMS maximum or a sum paid at admission | same (`feeBandText`) |

The levels are display only: `statCells` still decides every value, marker and "Unknown" by the same rules.

## Layout
- Astryx `AppShell` (`height="auto"`, `variant="section"`): the sticky `TopNav` carries the brand, the Compare tray button ("2/4", only when the tray has items; on phones it is a fourth item in the bottom bar) and "Back to site"; `SideNav` holds the rank "patient band" card plus 15 items in Plan (Overview, Options, Seats, Choices, Compare), Counselling (Schedule & notices), Research (Institutes, Last ranks, Allotments, Residents with a "Premium" badge, Rank scan, Rank journey, Score & rank, Fees & bond) and Help (Request a feature) groups (Lucide icons). Astryx provides the skip link, the main landmark and the mobile drawer. On phones there's also a bottom bar with Options, Seats, Choices and Rank scan (Compare "2/4" in its place while the tray has items). Nothing floats over the page, and `scroll-padding` (globals.css) sits above the header and bottom-bar heights, so a focused control is never hidden.
- The profile dialog renders inside `.app` (no portal), so it uses these tokens. With no profile saved or in the address it is the first-run setup: three steps (rank, category and PwD; courses as exact degree and specialty pairs; quotas), then Options, which opens on the current round's seats. `SetRankPrompt` starts it, and says "Loading" (never "set your rank") while a saved profile is read.
- Overview leads with what's open now (`RoundStatusBanner`, the Counselling page's status badges, from MCC notices) and her options as Options opens them (the round view while a round's seat lists are out): a stat strip and the label bars, each number linking to that Options view, then the history charts. 404 (`decide/not-found.tsx`), errors (`decide/error.tsx`) and "Data unavailable" (no database) render in the shell as an `EmptyState`.
- Content is left-aligned and divided by ruled hairlines and sheets, never floating shadowed cards. Headline numbers sit in one ruled **stat strip** (`StatStrip`), not as separate cards.
- Labels are sentence case. There are no all-caps eyebrows and no monospace labels.

## Charts (dataviz skill)
- Use the emphasis form: one accent (chart-ink blue) plus grey context. There are no categorical multi-hue series, because the palette validator failed our 4-hue set for colour-blind readers.
- Marks use `--color-mark` (`fill-mark`, `stroke-mark`, `bg-mark`); `sky` is for text and links. 2px lines, ≥8px markers with a 2px ring in the surface colour, bars at most 24px thick with a 4px rounded end. Grid lines are solid hairlines.
- Every chart has a legend when there are 2 or more series, selective direct labels, hover tooltips (value first, then label), and a "Show as table" view (`ChartCard`). A chart is one `role="img"` with a summary label; its marks are not tab stops (the strip has hundreds), the table view carries the numbers. Table views summarise (per course: count, deepest, median, shallowest) rather than list every option; the full list is the Options page.
- The memorable element is the **"Where your rank sits"** strip chart on faint ECG-style grid paper (`.chart-paper`), with the doctor's rank as the cursor line. Everything else stays quiet.
- Big standalone numbers use proportional figures. Table columns use `numeric` (tabular).

## Inside-app primitives (`src/features/decide/components`)
- `AppPage`: page frame (`min-w-0`; for grid pages use `grid grid-cols-1 gap-6`). `PageHead`: the h1 (`#main-h1`, focusable) and intro.
- `FilterBar` + `Field` + `Select` + `control` (ui.tsx): GET filter forms that keep the profile and the address's Cards | Table choice (`ViewField`); Apply keeps focus. `FilterDropdown`: multi-select pill (one open at a time, closes on Escape and focus-out). `MoreFilters` (FilterBar `more`; Options and Seats render it after their keyed fields): the one fold, on every filter bar: on phones the less-used fields (`more-filter`) fold behind "Show all filters", open when one of them is set; from md up every field is inline; it stays mounted, so her tick survives Apply. `TermFilterControls` (term-filter-controls.tsx, lib/term-filters.ts): college type, fee and bond years always in view, stipend, bond ₹, leaving ₹ and "Include not declared" `more-filter`, on Options, Seats, Allotments, Fees, Institutes, Last ranks and Rank scan alike; her profile's fee and bond-year caps apply on all of them (an absent `maxfee` / `bondyrs` is hers, "any" lifts it), and the default option and a chip say "Your cap: …". A filtered value that is not declared leaves its row out, counted ("N not declared left out: include them", `nd=keep`): see DATA_INTEGRITY "Unknown stays unknown". `ActiveFilters` (active-filters.tsx): under the bar, a chip per applied filter (each removes it in one click) and "Clear all" (Options and Seats leave her courses to the Course pill); after a removal focus moves to the chip now in its place (else the one before), with none left to the results heading.
- `ViewToggle` + `ViewPanes` (view-toggle.tsx, its client half view-toggle-client.tsx): Cards | Table on every results page, `layout=cards|table` in the address (never `view`: Options' and Seats' `view` is their own list view), saved per page on the device (and in a `medpath.view.<page>` cookie, so the server sends a returning visitor one pane: `chosenView`, server/view.ts); nothing chosen, cards below md and the table from md up, on every page (CSS picks the pane, so the first paint is right). Before hydration (or without JS) the toggle is two links, after it two `aria-pressed` buttons that switch in place. When the address (or that cookie) names `layout`, the server renders only that pane (half the HTML) and picking the other fetches the page again; otherwise both panes are in the page and only the one on show cites its documents in Sources (`SourcesOn`). The tools under the count are the same on every page: [Cards | Table][CSV] (`CsvLink` and `toolbarRow`, csv-link.tsx: one "CSV" button named "Download CSV of …", greyed with an (i) past the export's row cap; on phones on their own line under the count, from md up at its right). `ResultCard` (result-card.tsx): the compact card: header chips, the institute heading with its MCC profile icon link beside it (outside the heading, named "MCC profile: <institute>"), the course, a meta line "State • TypeBadge • Beds" (each mark wraps with the item after it, never left at a line's end), the stat cells as footer. `ResultCards`: the list under a heading with its count.
- Stat cells (`TermStatCells`, stat-cells.tsx, from `statCells` in lib/stat-format.ts): Fee | Stipend | Bond | Bond ₹ | Leaving as number cells from a row's `fees`, in that order everywhere. `card` (default): a ruled strip, short values ("₹50 L", read out in full), each cell with its (i) (its full value, the exact words, other years, source numbers). `table`: five `<td>`s (`data-col` = the key) with full values and an (i) each (Options, Fees, Choices, Rank journey, the college's pages, Compare via `StatCellView`); `short` gives a wide table the cards' values (Seats). `compact`: short values with no (i) per cell, under `StatHead` headers (one (i) a column), for the longest tables (Allotments, Last ranks, Rank scan). Table cells break only after a range's dash and put the marker on its own line. Tables put the cells right after the course (and quota). Values are coloured by their level (Colour key). Values: "—" not in the data, "See text" words only, "Unknown" a record with no clear answer, "No bond" / Bond ₹ "None" only where the college's document or the NBEMS rule says so (a no-bond document where a state law's service may apply is "Unknown", marked "state law may apply" in sun), a bond in orange (`text-sun`). Markers: "max" / "min" for the NBEMS limits (never the college's own figure), "above NBEMS max" / "below NBEMS min" (with the NBEMS figure and its source where the record gives it) and "GST removed" (Seatora's reading of a declared figure, the declared one beside it in the (i); a fee cap between the two counts it as unknown) in sun, "at admission", "NMC list", "+ DA", "NBEMS rule", "caveat", "state rule" (the years are the state rule's: its record, or the document's summary marking them so, with a warning line) / "+ state rule" (a state rule as well, the document's own years), and on Bond ₹ "by specialty" (also a record priced by clinical vs pre/para-clinical subject, for a course in neither list), "computed" (per year × years), "incl. additional". ## Adding inside-app UI
Prefer Astryx components (`npx @astryxdesign/cli component <Name>` for docs): Decide uses AppShell, SideNav, TopNav and EmptyState; tables, selects and filter bars are the Seatora primitives above. Where an existing Seatora component already fits, reuse it; the scoped tokens restyle it. Astryx CSS is imported only in `src/app/decide/layout.tsx`, but it stays loaded after a client navigation back to the site, so `globals.css` restates the site's `:root` border and colour scheme after the Astryx layers. Buttons: see [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) (md and icon are 44px).
