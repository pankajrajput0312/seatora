# Product

## Vision
The most trustworthy place to research postgraduate medical admissions in India. Every number is sourced, every opinion is labelled, and nothing is presented as more certain than it is.

## Target users
- NEET PG aspirants before the exam who are estimating outcomes.
- Doctors with a rank who are preparing for AIQ (MCC) and state counselling.
- Candidates in DNB, deemed and central institute counselling.
- Medical students researching specialties and colleges ahead of time.

## Core problems
1. Cutoff data is scattered across PDFs from MCC and dozens of state authorities, in inconsistent formats.
2. Fees, stipends and bond terms are hard to find, change often and are rarely dated.
3. What residency is actually like (patient load, work hours, academics) is only available through informal networks.
4. Counselling notices are spread across many portals, and missing one can cost a seat.
5. Choice filling is high-stakes and usually done from spreadsheets and guesswork.

## Positioning
A research tool, not a coaching or marketing site. It is calm, dense with information and explicit about sources. It does not promise admissions.

## Core user journeys
1. **Discover**: search or filter colleges by state, type and specialty, then open a profile.
2. **Research**: read a college profile covering specialties, seat matrix, cutoffs, fees, stipend, bond, clinical exposure and resident experiences, each with sources.
3. **Compare**: put 2–4 colleges side by side, optionally for one specialty.
4. **Rank-based discovery**: enter rank, category, quota and preferences to see colleges whose *historical* closing ranks are near the rank.
5. **Stay updated**: follow announcements by authority and category.
6. **Shortlist**: save colleges and build an ordered choice list (requires an account).

## Main modules
Colleges · Cutoffs · Seat matrix · Fees/Stipend/Bond · Reviews · Comparison · Rank discovery · Announcements · Student profile.

## MVP scope
The public website, college discovery, profiles, cutoff explorer, comparison and announcements, built on a small, clearly sourced initial dataset. No accounts in the MVP.
