# Seatora walkthrough: Round 2 choice filling

A plain-language tour of every screen in Seatora Decide, set up for one profile: **AIR 42,284, OBC**, courses **Anaesthesiology (MD, DNB, NBEMS Diploma, Diploma)** and **DNB Family Medicine**, MCC All India counselling 2026. Any other profile works the same way: enter your own rank, category and courses.

**MCC 2026 Round 2 choice filling closes on 15 Nov at 23:55 IST.** Seatora helps you choose. It does not submit anything to MCC: only the choices saved and locked on the MCC portal count. Confirm every rule and date on mcc.nic.in.

## Three things to keep in mind

1. **History, not prediction.** Every rank on screen is a closing rank from a past MCC result: the last rank allotted in that round on that seat type. Labels like "Reached in 2026 R1" say what happened then. Seats, categories and competition change every round, so no label means you will or won't get a seat.
2. **Hospital facts are self-reported.** Beds, patient numbers and facilities are what each hospital published about itself (its website, NIRF, NMC or NBEMS pages). Seatora hasn't checked them. "—" means not found, not zero.
3. **Fees are in the official words when there is no single figure.** A number is shown only where the college's MCC profile gives one clearly labelled amount per year. Otherwise Seatora shows the profile's own fee wording with its link, never a guessed figure. "NBEMS maximum" and "NBEMS minimum" are NBEMS's limits for DNB hospitals, not the hospital's own figures.

## Getting in and setting your profile

1. Open https://www.seatora.co.in/decide and sign in with the invited account. Decide (everything below) is private to that account.
2. The first time, Decide asks three things. Your answers are saved on this device:
   - **Rank and category:** All India Rank `42284`, Category `OBC`, PwD unticked.
   - **Courses:** type `anaes` and tick **Anaesthesiology**, then type `family` and tick **Family Medicine**. Under Degrees, keep all four for Anaesthesiology (MD, DNB, Diploma, NBEMS Diploma). For Family Medicine, untick MD and NBEMS Diploma so only **DNB** stays.
   - **Quotas:** tick nothing. That means All India, DNB Quota and Deemed/Paid Seats, the quotas anyone can take. Tick a restricted quota (Armed Forces, NRI, minority, a university's own) only if you are eligible for it.
3. **Save and see your options** opens Options. The sidebar now shows "Your rank 42,284, OBC, 5 courses" with **Edit**. Use Edit later to add States, a Max fee per year or a Max bond.

Good to know:
- The profile goes into page addresses, so a link you share carries your rank and category.
- To use the same profile on your phone, choose **Edit → Export JSON** here, then **Import JSON** there. The choice list can't be moved that way: it stays in the browser where you built it. Build it on one device and keep its CSV as a backup (see Choices).
- On a phone, the bar at the bottom has Options, Seats and Choice list, and Compare once you've added something to compare. Every other screen is in the menu.

## A plan for today

1. **Home:** see where Round 2 stands and how many Round 2 seats are in your courses.
2. **Options:** go through the Round 2 view, label by label, and star what you would accept.
3. **Seats:** check seat counts and every past closing rank for the rows you starred.
4. **College pages:** read the fees, bond, hospital facts and residents before deciding.
5. **Compare:** line up two to four close calls side by side.
6. **Choice list:** put the list in the order you want the seats, then type it into the MCC portal, save and lock it before 23:55 IST.

## Home

Home is your summary page:
- **Where the round stands.** It shows, for example, "Open now: Round 2 choice filling until 15 Nov 23:55 IST" and "Next: Round 2 provisional result on 16 Nov", each linked to its MCC notice. The status comes from MCC's notices and shows the time it was read. Check the MCC site before acting on it.
- **Round 2 seats in your courses.** On 15 Nov this was about 1,000 options with seats in MCC's Round 2 lists for this profile, about two thirds of them in the clear vacancy list. It also shows how many reached your rank in a past round and how many came within 10%. Every number opens those options on Options.
- **Options by label, Where your rank sits, How closing ranks moved through 2025.** Charts of the same options: how many fall under each label, and where your rank sits among the 2025 closing ranks.
- **Your Round 2 list.** How many options you've starred, and how many you've typed into MCC.

## Options (the Round 2 view)

This is the main working screen. Each row is one option: an institute, a course and a quota.

**The summary at the top** gives the count of options with Round 2 seats in your courses, split into clear vacancy, virtual only and newly added. It links the three MCC PDFs, with their dates (clear and virtual vacancy 13 Nov, newly added seats 12 Nov).

**What the three seat lists mean.** Their seats are never added together, because a seat can be on two lists.
- **Clear vacancy:** seats nobody holds after Round 1.
- **Virtual vacancy:** seats someone holds now, which become free only if that person upgrades or leaves in Round 2. They are real options, but less certain.
- **Newly added seats:** seats added after the seat matrix. Most are also in the clear vacancy list.

**The labels (chips above the table).** Pick one to see only those options. They compare your rank with closing ranks on **Open or OBC seats** (the seat categories you can take) in 2024, 2025 and 2026 R1:
- **Reached in 2026 R1:** this year's Round 1 closed at or beyond 42,284 here.
- **Reached in 2024 and 2025** / **Reached in 2024 or 2025:** it closed at or beyond your rank in some round of both years, or of one year.
- **Within 10% in a past round:** it closed a little before your rank (within about 4,200 ranks).
- **Closed before your rank:** every counted round closed before 42,284. The Round 2 view still shows these rows, because Round 2 seats can go deeper. Use "Hide them" to hide them.
- **Never allotted before:** MCC never allotted this course and quota here, so there is no history to compare. Judge it by the college.
- **No allotment in your categories:** past allotments here were only on other seat categories.

**Each row shows** the label, the Round 2 seats in each list, the 2026 R1 closing rank, the deepest 2025 and 2024 closing ranks (with the round and seat category), and a one-line fee, stipend, bond and leaving-penalty summary. Its **Details** link to Rounds by year (every round of every year), Last ranks, Allotments and Fees & bond for that option.

**Filters worth using.**
- **Quota.** Deemed/Paid Seats close very deep, so they fill the top of the list, and their fees are much higher. To see government and NBEMS seats first, tick only All India and DNB Quota.
- **Max fee per year** and **Max bond**, for your limits.
- **State**, and search by institute, state or course.
- **Seats in the 2026 R2 lists**, for example "Has 2026 R2 clear vacancy seats" to see only the surest seats.

**Actions:**
- The **star** adds an option to your Round 2 choice list.
- **Compare** adds it to the compare tray (up to four).
- **Download these options (CSV)** saves the current list for a spreadsheet.

"Every option with past allotments instead" switches to the other view: all options with history, closed ones hidden, without the Round 2 seat focus.

## Seats (the seat matrix)

Seats shows MCC's seat lists exactly as each PDF printed them. The default is the **Seat matrix**, which has one row per institute, course, quota and **seat category** (so Open and OBC seats at one college are separate rows). For this profile on 15 Nov it showed about 1,200 rows.

Each row gives:
- the Round 2 **clear** and **virtual** seats, with a **+N new** badge when the newly added list also prints seats there (not extra seats);
- the course's fee, stipend, bond and leaving penalty;
- the beds the hospital reports (self-reported);
- **Your chances**, which is the same history label as on Options, here for that one seat category only;
- the **closing rank in every round** from 2023 to 2026 R1, with the number of allotments MCC made in brackets. These are allotments made in that round, not the number of seats.

Ways to use it:
- Scroll sideways for older rounds. Select a row or its **Details** for every number and its source. **Display** hides columns you don't need.
- **Sort** by your chances, deepest past closing, most seats, institute, state, lowest fee or shortest bond.
- The **Seat category** filter starts on your categories (Open, OBC). You can also show all of them.
- **All rounds** adds rows from earlier rounds' lists. **One list** shows a single PDF. **Compare the 2026 R2 lists** puts the three lists side by side.
- The **Allotment trends** tab shows allotments made per year in your courses (allotments, not seats).
- Under the counts, the **Official** notes link each PDF. Read them: MCC's 13 Nov notice adding and removing Round 2 seats came after the newly added list, so a seat it changed may not be applied here. Check that notice before you lock.

## College pages

Open any institute name. The header gives the address as printed by MCC, the type, the official MCC profile link and the website, where declared. The type (Government, Private, Deemed and so on) is sometimes *inferred* from the name and quota, and the page says so when it is. The page has seven tabs:

- **Overview:** your options here with their labels and deepest 2025 closing ranks; a short fees, stipend and bond line; allotments made by year; and allotments by quota and seat category.
- **Courses:** every course and quota here with its label, latest closing rank and deepest 2025 closing rank.
- **Seats:** this college's rows in each Round 2 list, by course, quota and seat category.
- **Allotments:** every allotment here, newest first: rank, round, seat and candidate category, status. Ranks only, never names.
- **Fees & bond:** one section per programme (MD/MS, DNB…) and seat type.
  - **Fee and stipend by year** of the course, each figure numbered to its source document.
  - Whether the fee includes hostel, and hostel availability, when the MCC profile says.
  - **The service bond:** years, amount, who it applies to and its exact words.
  - **Leaving the course after joining:** the penalty, which can be a state rule, labelled as such.
  - **What MCC lets the college keep if you resign or don't join from Round 2,** separate from the leaving penalty.

  For example, a Maharashtra government college shows a 1-year bond of ₹50 lakh and a ₹10 lakh leaving penalty, plus another ₹10 lakh if the seat lapses, each linked to its document. A DNB hospital shows the NBEMS fee maximum, ₹1.25 lakh a year with no GST, and the NBEMS stipend minimum.
- **Hospital facts (as reported):** beds, ICU, OPD/IPD, theatres and similar facts, each with its period, where Seatora read it and when. It also notes whether the NBEMS mandatory disclosure applies. All of it is self-reported. Many cells are "—" because the hospital hasn't published that figure.
- **Residents:** people admitted here in 2023 to 2025, from MCC's official admitted-candidates lists. You see a count by course and year, then the names, with your courses first. The rank ("AIR 12,345 · 2024") is that person's All India Rank in that year's NBEMS NEET PG result, where known. It shows where one person got in, not a closing rank. Seatora has no phone numbers or other contact details. If you reach out to residents (for example on Instagram), be respectful. **Hide** removes a name for good. Names are shown only to the signed-in invited account.

## Fees & bond (all colleges)

The same fee, stipend and bond data as the college pages, for every institute at once. Filter by programme (your profile's by default), state, institute, max fee per year, min stipend in year 1, max bond years, max bond amount, and bond declared or not, then sort by fee. A dash means not in Seatora's MCC data. It never means zero or "no bond". "Not checked yet" means Seatora has no bond record for that programme, and a government college may still have a state service bond.

## Choice list

Your Choice list is your list, in the order you will type it into the MCC portal (every screen calls it Choice list; Ask Seatora's saved list is "Draft from Ask Seatora"). It is named **Choice list (Round 2 · 2026)**, and every starred option lands here.

- **Reorder** by dragging, with the keyboard (Space to pick up, arrows to move, Space to drop), with the up and down buttons, or by typing a position number and pressing **Move**. **Undo** brings back a removed row.
- Each row shows its current label and its Round 2 seats. Rows that no longer have seats, or are closed before your rank, are flagged. "Same institute and course listed more than once" warns about duplicates.
- **Copy in MCC order** copies "1. Institute — Course — Quota" lines. **Download CSV** (each choice's latest and deepest closing, fee, stipend, bond and leaving penalty too) and **Print** save it. **Share on WhatsApp** sends the list with a link to it. Keep a copy, because the list lives in this browser only.
- **Typed into the MCC portal up to #**: as you enter choices on the MCC portal, set this number. Seatora then warns if you move a row that is already typed in ("Check the order in the MCC portal").
- **Duplicate for next round** copies the list as the start of the next round's list.
- **Suggest my Choice list** puts your options in one suggested order, each with its reason ("Dream: closed at 23,502 in 2025 R3, 6% before you"): your course order first (Edit, **Your order**: move courses up or down, tick **Equal** for ones you'd take as gladly), then Dream, Target and Safe within each course, within your fee, bond and college-type limits (what they leave out is counted). It comes from past closing ranks, not a prediction. **Add all** or **Add selected** puts them after your choices; **Use this order** puts them first and keeps yours after. Nothing is removed. Trying a rank without saving: Copy or CSV.

Order the list by what you want most, not by your chances. Then type it into the MCC portal, **save and lock it there** before 23:55 IST.

## Compare

Up to four options side by side. Add them with the compare button on Options, your Choice list or a college page. The rows are:
- course, quota and state;
- your label;
- closing ranks by year and round, on Open or OBC seats, each saying which;
- allotments made per year;
- fee, stipend, bond and leaving penalty.

Clinical load says "Not collected yet". Use each college's Hospital facts tab for that.

## Counselling: schedule and notices

- **Schedule:** every 2026 round step (seat matrix, choice filling, provisional result, final result), with its status and the MCC notice behind it. Dates are as printed, and tentative ones may change.
- **Announcements:** every MCC notice in the data, newest first, filterable by round.
- **Merit lists:** the NEET PG results Seatora uses.

## Research screens

- **Institutes:** every institute in the MCC results, searchable by name, state and course, with 2025 allotment counts. Open one for its college page.
- **Last ranks:** pick a course, a seat category and a quota. Each seat shows its Best (the deepest closing in any year and round, with its year and round); Rounds by year (or Expand all) shows the closing rank in every round of every year, with the allotments made in brackets.
- **Allotments:** every allotment in the MCC results, filterable by year, round, course and more. Each shows rank, quota, seat and candidate category, choice number, status (allotted, upgraded…) and the seat held a round earlier. Ranks only, never names.
- **Rank scan:** allotments to ranks near yours (± 100, 250 or 500) in one year, every course and category. It shows what ranks like yours got then.
- **Rank journey:** one rank's seat in every round of one year: the seat, MCC's status, the choice number, and the seat held a round earlier. Useful to see how upgrades played out.
- **Score & rank:** what a NEET PG score meant as a rank in earlier NBEMS results (2026 is out of 720, 2025 out of 800, so compare ranks, not scores). **Merit position** shows how many OBC candidates were at or ahead of your rank in the 2025 AIQ merit list. That was 14,594 to 14,962 of 49,047 for AIR 42,284. Counts from that year only.

## The honest caveats, in full

- **No predictions.** Labels and closing ranks are history: MCC's past results on the seat category shown. The seat matrix, categories, quotas and competition change every round.
- **"Closing rank" belongs to the seat's category, not the candidate's.** As an OBC candidate you can take Open and OBC seats. Seatora shows the deeper of the two and says which one it is.
- **Counts in brackets are allotments made in that round, not seats.** From Round 2 on, they count only fresh and upgraded allotments.
- **Seat lists are as MCC printed them.** Later MCC notices that add or remove seats are linked, not applied. Virtual vacancy seats open only if their holder moves.
- **2026 R1 data comes from MCC's final result.** Where a result is provisional, Seatora marks it "Provisional".
- **Hospital facts are self-reported** by each hospital and not verified by Seatora. "—" means not found, not zero.
- **Fees come in the official words when there is no single figure.** A number appears only where the MCC profile gives one clearly labelled amount per year. NBEMS maximum and minimum are limits, not a hospital's figures. "Not declared" means not in the MCC profile fields Seatora reads, not "none".
- **Bonds:** "unknown" or "Not checked yet" means no bond document was checked, not that there is no bond. A state rule is shown as the state's rule, with its order linked.
- **College type is sometimes inferred** from the name and quota, and is labelled when it is.
- **Residents** come from MCC's admitted-candidates lists. They show where people were admitted, with no contact details. Some 2025 Stray-round joiners have no public name.
- **Your profile and choices are saved in this browser.** Export the profile and download the list before switching devices. The MCC portal is the only place your choices count.
