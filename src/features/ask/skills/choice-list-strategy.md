# Choice list strategy (principles)

1. **Order by preference, not by probability.** MCC allots the highest choice available at her rank, so a "dream" choice above a realistic one costs nothing. Put what she would truly prefer first.
2. **Dream → realistic → safe.** Within her real preferences: a few dream options (closed a little before her rank in recent rounds), then many realistic ones (reached in the latest year's rounds so far, or in 2025), then enough safe ones (reached comfortably in both past years) that she is not left without a seat.
3. **Course priority first, then college.** If she would take DNB Anaesthesia anywhere before MD Family Medicine anywhere, all of the first come before the second. If a strong college matters more than the course, interleave on purpose.
4. **Money and bond limits are filters, not preferences.** Remove options she cannot pay for or whose bond/leaving penalty she will not accept, before ordering. Unknown (not published) terms: keep only if she accepts the risk, and say so.
5. **Never fill a seat she would not join.** From Round 2 on, not joining forfeits the deposit; Round 3 cannot be resigned. Every line must be a seat she would actually take.
6. **No gaps.** Too short a list risks no allotment; include enough realistic and safe options. The newest seat lists (clear/virtual/new) are the ones of the round they were published for: once that round's result is out they are history, and the next round's lists replace them.
7. **Home state and location**: state quota counselling runs in parallel; AIQ choices in her home state may compete with state options; she decides.
8. **Copy exactly.** MCC lists institute + course + quota; two colleges can share a name, so use the PIN/state. Seatora's Choices page (/decide/choices) has "Copy in MCC order".
9. **Re-check each round**: new seat lists, new closing ranks, changed fees. Lock before the deadline (`schedule` tool).
