# DNB vs MD (factual, balanced)

Sources: Seatora's research guide (NMC faculty regulations 2025 via medicaldialogues.in; NBEMS curricula and bulletins on natboard.edu.in / nbe.edu.in; NBEMS stipend revision via careers360). Say "Seatora's research" and give the link when you cite these.

- **Recognition**: NMC's 2025 faculty regulations treat DNB as equivalent to MD/MS. DNB from a hospital with fewer than 500 beds needs 1 extra year of senior residency in a recognised medical college before becoming an assistant professor.
- **Training**: MD is in medical colleges (NMC curriculum; residents also do the 3-month District Residency Programme). DNB is in NBEMS-accredited hospitals; a department missing a sub-speciality may rotate trainees elsewhere for up to 6 months under an MoU. Caseload and supervision vary by place: government colleges often give volume with thinner supervision, corporate DNB hospitals closer consultant supervision with fewer cases (an inference, not measured). Check with current residents.
- **Exams**: DNB needs a logbook, thesis and Formative Assessment Tests before the final; results are pass/fail and NBEMS publishes no institute-wise pass rates. MD exams are by the university.
- **Fees**: DNB at most ₹1.25 L a year (NBEMS). Government MD is usually low; private and deemed MD can be ₹ lakhs to tens of lakhs a year - use the tools for the college's figure.
- **Stipend**: DNB minimum ₹43k/45k/47k a month (years 1-3) from Jun 2025, or the state rate if higher. MD stipends follow state/college rates; some private colleges pay below the state rate.
- **Bonds**: private DNB hospitals may not take a bond or deposit; government DNB hospitals only if declared before counselling. Government MD seats often carry state service bonds and leaving penalties (see fees-stipend-bond).
- **Accreditation risk**: NBEMS can withdraw seats if faculty leave (e.g. Jan 2025, DNB General Surgery seats at one Kolkata hospital); a department with 1-2 seats has 1-2 qualified faculty.

Present both sides; let her weigh them. No guarantees about careers or exam outcomes.
