# MCC NEET PG counselling basics

Primary source: MCC Information Bulletin NEET-PG 2026 (https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) and the MCC schedule notice (https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610081115756031.pdf). Rules change every year: for anything that decides money or eligibility, tell her to confirm in the current bulletin. Live dates: call the `schedule` tool, never quote dates from memory.

## Rounds (2026, as Seatora's research read the bulletin)
- Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, then the Stray vacancy round. Each round: seat matrix, choice filling and locking, provisional result, final result, reporting/joining.
- New for 2026: **Freeze** = accept the seat and join physically; **Float** = join online, upload documents and ask for an upgrade (no visit). Upgrades through Float are possible only up to Round 3.
- **Round 1 has a free exit**: she may skip joining a Round 1 seat and still take part in Round 2. If she takes a Round 1 seat as Freeze without asking for an upgrade, she is not considered in Round 2.
- **Round 2 and later**: not joining an allotted seat forfeits the deposit. Round 1 and 2 seats can be resigned within the window.
- **Round 3 is final**: once she joins a Round 3 seat she cannot upgrade or resign it. Not joining loses the deposit and shuts her out of further MCC rounds.
- **Stray round**: only for candidates not holding a seat. Anyone on a state's post-Round-3 list is removed from the AIQ stray round.
- Choices carry forward only as MCC says for that round; in each round she fills and locks choices again on the MCC portal.

## Deposit (refundable security, MCC 2026)
₹25,000 (UR/EWS, AIQ), ₹10,000 (OBC/SC/ST/PwD, AIQ), ₹2,00,000 (deemed universities). Forfeited if she does not join a seat allotted in Round 2 or later, or her admission is cancelled. MCC sets no bond: bond, fee and stipend terms are the institute's (MCC disclaims them).

## Quotas (MCC labels, exactly as Seatora's data prints them)
- **All India**: the AIQ share of government medical college seats plus central institutions; open to all.
- **Deemed/Paid Seats**: deemed universities (all their PG seats are filled through MCC); higher fees and the ₹2 L deposit.
- **DNB Quota**: NBEMS DNB and NBEMS Diploma seats at accredited hospitals, filled through MCC.
- Restricted quotas, only for those eligible: Armed Forces Medical (in-service), Non-Resident Indian, Muslim Minority / Jain Minority, Delhi University, IP University, Banaras Hindu University, Aligarh Muslim University (their own graduates). Seatora's default ("open quotas") is All India, DNB Quota and Deemed/Paid Seats.
- AIIMS, JIPMER, PGIMER and similar INIs admit through INI-CET, not NEET PG MCC counselling.

## Categories
- Seat categories in MCC results: Open, OBC, EWS, SC, ST, each also as PwD. A candidate may be allotted an Open seat on merit; an OBC candidate is eligible for Open and OBC seats (and PwD seats if PwD).
- OBC needs a central-list NCL certificate in MCC's format (Annexure-4 in 2026), income for the year ending 31 Mar 2026; a state-format certificate risks losing the OBC seat.
- Private DNB hospitals have no category reservation, so OBC status helps only at AIQ, central and government DNB seats.
