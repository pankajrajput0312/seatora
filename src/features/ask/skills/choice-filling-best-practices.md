# Choice filling: best practices (MCC AIQ)

Sources: MCC Information Bulletin NEET-PG 2026 (https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf), Seatora's research guide. Live dates: the `schedule` tool.

1. **Fill many choices.** There is no penalty for a long list: MCC goes down it and allots the first seat available at her rank. A short list is the main way to end a round with nothing.
2. **Order strictly by true preference, never by chance.** Put first what she would most like to join. A seat's chance does not change with its position; only her preference should.
3. **Long shots go above likely ones.** She gets at most one allotment per round. A long shot placed below a likely seat can never be allotted, because the likely seat is taken first. So dream choices sit at the top, then realistic, then safe.
4. **Never fill a seat she would not join.** Round 1 has a free exit (she may skip joining and still take part in Round 2). From Round 2 on, not joining an allotted seat forfeits the deposit (₹25,000 UR/EWS, ₹10,000 OBC/SC/ST/PwD, ₹2 L deemed), and a joined Round 3 seat can't be resigned or upgraded.
5. **Upgrades.** Joining with Float (online) keeps her in the next round for better choices, up to Round 3; Freeze means she is done. In the next round she lists only seats she prefers to the one she holds; if nothing better comes, she keeps hers.
6. **Round 3 and stray, realistically.** Seats left after Round 2 are fewer; Round 3 closings are often deeper because of upgrades, but a Round 3 seat is final. The stray round is only for those without a seat and fills leftovers: never plan to rely on it.
7. **Check money and bond before filling.** Fee per year (× years when all are stated), stipend, service bond (years and penalty), leaving penalty, and MCC's forfeit. "Not published" means unknown: ask the college, or leave it out if the risk is too big.
8. **Keep a state-quota plan.** State counselling runs in parallel; keep her state's registration and choices ready as a backup, and know which round's seat she would give up.
9. **Lock before the deadline.** Save often; lock choices well before the last hour (portals slow down). Check the provisional result and report or join within the window.
10. **Re-check every round.** New seat lists, new closings, changed fees.

## How to use this in an answer
End a strategy answer with a short checklist from these points that fit her case (3-6 bullets), not all ten every time.
