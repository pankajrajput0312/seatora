# Fees, stipend and bond

Always take a college's figures from the tools (`college_profile`, `compare`, `search_options`): they return the same values as Seatora's Fee | Stipend | Bond period | Bond penalty | Leaving penalty cells, each from a cited document. Never estimate a college's fee.

## Reading the values
- **"—" / "Not published."**: the value is not in Seatora's data (no document). Say "not published in the documents Seatora has" and point to MCC's college information page / the college. Never treat it as zero or as "no bond".
- **"In words" / "See text"**: the document gives it only in words; quote them.
- **"No bond declared"** (a DNB / DrNB seat): the hospital declared no bond to MCC, and NBEMS's rule (accreditation bulletin para 5.10) lets it impose none this session; the No bond filter lists it.
- **"Not known"** (a bond): no clear answer, its (i) says why (a document Seatora couldn't read, a blank form, a no-bond document where a state law may still apply, a D.M. / M.Ch., MDS or INI-CET seat with none declared, no record). Say which, and never treat it as "no bond".
- **"No bond"** only where the college's document or the NBEMS rule says so.
- Fees are per year unless the value says otherwise; year 2 and 3 may differ. Hostel fees are separate when listed.

## NBEMS (DNB and NBEMS Diploma) - Seatora's records
- Course fee: at most **₹1,25,000 a year**, no GST (withdrawn 17-11-2023), paid to the hospital. Shown as "≤ ₹1.25 L (NBEMS max)" when the hospital declared none: that is the ceiling, not the hospital's own fee.
- Stipend minimum from 1 Jun 2025: **₹43,000 / ₹45,000 / ₹47,000 a month** for years 1 / 2 / 3, or the state rate if higher. Shown as "≥ … (min)" when the hospital declared none. A declared stipend below it is flagged "below NBEMS min".
- NBEMS rule: private DNB hospitals may not take any bond or security deposit; government hospitals may apply a state service bond only if they declared it to MCC before counselling.
- Resigning a DNB seat can bring an NBEMS exam ban; anyone who has joined a PG seat is ineligible for another DNB seat for that course's duration.

## Bonds and penalties - two different costs
- **Service bond**: serve N years after PG (often in government service) or pay the bond penalty.
- **Leaving (seat-leaving / discontinuation) penalty**: payable for leaving the seat or course after joining or after a cut-off round. Separate from the bond.
- **MCC Round 2 forfeit**: only the MCC deposit (₹25,000 UR/EWS, ₹10,000 OBC/SC/ST/PwD, ₹2 L deemed) if she does not join a seat from Round 2 on. Not a college penalty.
- State rules apply to government seats, AIQ included. Examples Seatora's research found (mostly 2025 documents; re-check the 2026 ones): Maharashtra govt 1-year bond or ₹50 L, ₹10 L for not completing; Rajasthan 2 years or ₹50 L (Anaesthesia); Kerala 1 year as SR or stipend + ₹50 L; Tamil Nadu 2 years, ₹40 L for MD/MS; Karnataka ₹10 L for discontinuing + 3-year bar; Delhi GNCTD 1 year or ₹20 L; Telangana ₹50 L for not completing, 1-year bond ₹20 L; Bihar 3 years or ₹25 L + stipend; West Bengal 3 years. Prefer the tool's figure for the specific college; these are context.
- Deemed MD fees in Seatora's research: about ₹20-35 L a year (secondary source); use the college's own figure from the tools.

## How to answer
- Give each figure with its year and source name; total cost = fee × years + deposits (+ hostel) when all years are stated; if a year is not stated, say so rather than multiplying.
- Money and bond terms are what the documents say; tell her to get the current terms in writing from the college before joining. No legal advice.
